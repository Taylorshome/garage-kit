Your cars' research files go here, one folder per car (`research/<car-slug>/research.json`, `research.md`,
`parts-guides.json`, `fluids.json`, `brands.json`). This folder is git-ignored: it is your data, not part of the kit.
Load with `node scripts/load-research.mjs --car <nickname> --dir research/<car-slug> --dry-run`. Schema: docs/research-schema.md.
