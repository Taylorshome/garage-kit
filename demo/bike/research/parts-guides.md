# 2007 Yamaha V Star 650 (XVS650 / A / AT): Parts, Guides, Tools

Research date **Nov 24, 2026**. **Reduced scope** (demo/test vehicle): 8 parts, 3 guides, 3 tools. Prices mostly not checked. RevZilla, manualowl, AMSOIL and the Yamaha EU manual PDFs returned 403 to automated fetch and were skipped. Data source: `parts-guides.json`.

## Short list

| Need | Buy | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Oil | JASO MA motorcycle oil (Yamalube 10W-40 / 20W-40), **2.8 L (2.96 qt) with filter**, 2.6 L without | capacity from 2011 manual; viscosity chart verify |
| Oil filter | **HiFlo HF145** / **K&N KN-145** (cartridge) | good. OEM number unconfirmed (4X7-13440-xx vs 5GH-13440-71-00): look it up on a fiche |
| Plugs (2) | **NGK DPR7EA-9** (iridium DPR7EIX-9), gap ~0.035 in | gap verify |
| Battery | **Yuasa YT12B-BS** (not YTX14-BS) | verify against the installed battery |
| Air filter | OEM **4TR-14451-00-00** / **K&N YA-6598** | K&N cross-ref |
| Regulator/rectifier | OEM **5KP-81960-00-00**; RMStator RMS020-103273; Shindengen FH020AA kit (needs adapters) | test charging first |
| Final gear oil | SAE 80 API GL-4 hypoid (80W-90 GL-4), **~0.19 L** | verify |
| Front pads | EBC **FA179** / FA179V | J&P/Cycle Gear fitment |

**Wet clutch:** never use energy-conserving car oil, because it can make the clutch slip.

## Tools
- **Carb sync:** Morgan Carbtune Pro or Motion Pro SyncPro, or a DIY manometer.
- **Valve adjust:** Motion Pro tappet tool, metric feeler gauges and an in-lb torque wrench.
- **Battery maintainer:** Battery Tender Jr or NOCO Genius.

Prices were not checked.

## Additives
- Stabilizer for storage: **use**.
- Ring Free / Seafoam: **maybe**.
- Oil friction additives: **skip**, because of the wet clutch.
